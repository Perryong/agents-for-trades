## Gap Details

### SCORE-03: Confidence-Calibration Chart (PARTIAL)

**What exists:** Backend `GET /api/scores/calibration` endpoint fully implemented with 5 confidence buckets. Frontend `CalibrationChart.tsx` renders lightweight-charts v5 line series with perfect-calibration diagonal. Both are tested and compile clean.

**What's broken:** `confidence_text` field is missing from the frontend `TradeRequest` TypeScript interface (`frontend/src/types.ts:126-136`). The `submitTrade()` call in `ChartScreen.tsx:93-99` never includes `overlay.final_trade_decision` as `confidence_text`. Consequently, the `confidence` column in the Trade SQLite table is always `null` for every trade submitted through the browser.

**Impact:** The calibration chart permanently shows "Need 10+ scored trades for calibration data" because no trade ever has a non-null confidence value.

**Fix (2 lines):**
1. Add `confidence_text?: string` to `TradeRequest` in `frontend/src/types.ts`
2. Add `confidence_text: overlay?.final_trade_decision` to the `submitTrade({...})` call in `ChartScreen.tsx`
